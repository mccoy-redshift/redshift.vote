Privacy Policy

Red Shift · Last updated October 5, 2026

Red Shift (redshift.vote) is a volunteer app for grassroots political campaigns. This policy says what Red Shift collects, how it is used, and who sees it.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name and email address. Red Shift does not receive your Google password or anything else in your Google account.
  • What you enter: registration details (such as name, phone, address and town), inquiries you send, and your settings.
  • Your volunteer work: the walks, calls, texts, events and other activity you record in the app, such as doors knocked and hours worked.
  • Campaign data: voter and canvassing records that campaigns load into the app to organize their outreach.

How it is used

Only to run Red Shift: signing you in, organizing volunteers and campaigns, assigning and tracking work, and showing progress on the app's dashboards.

Who sees it

Your information is not sold, and it is not shared outside Red Shift. Within the app, it is visible to the people who need it for the work, according to their role (for example, your campaign's administrators). On dashboards, volunteers appear as "Volunteer 1, 2, …" unless they choose to show their name.

Where it is kept

Red Shift runs on Google services (Google Apps Script, Sheets and Drive), and its data is stored there.

Your choices

You can change your settings in the app, or ask us to correct or remove your information at the email below.
