More FAQ on Absentee Voting
When does my ballot have to arrive?
  • Deadline: received by 8 p.m., Tue, Nov. 3.
Can I vote in person instead?
  • Yes, at early voting or your polling place on Nov. 3, if you haven’t returned it.
  • Hand in your absentee ballot, or have it spoiled and get a new one.
Who can return my ballot for me?
  • You.
  • Immediate family or someone in your household, if you ask.
  • A mail carrier.
  • An election official.
  • Anyone else holding or delivering it commits a felony.
I made a mistake or signed in the wrong place.
  • Ask your clerk for a new ballot.
  • Sign as on your driver’s license, state ID or voter registration.
How do I know my ballot arrived?
  • Call your clerk.
  • Check for updates: Michigan.gov/Vote
Can I change my vote after returning it?
  • Only by a written, signed request.
  • Deadline: received by 5 p.m., Fri, Oct. 23.
  • Check for updates: Michigan Secretary of State
