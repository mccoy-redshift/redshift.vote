Where to mail

Sign and date the return envelope the way you sign your driver’s license or state ID, and mail it in the prepaid envelope. It must reach your clerk by 8 p.m. on Nov. 3. The state advises mailing at least two weeks before Election Day (by Oct. 20); after that, use a drop box or your clerk’s office.

Where is my drop box

Your clerk’s secure drop box takes your signed envelope until 8 p.m. on Nov. 3. Clerks collect from it and store the ballots safely until counting.

Where is my clerk’s office

Hand your signed envelope to your clerk in person during office hours, or on the weekend before Election Day when the office is open.

Where is my early voting location

Bring your completed ballot to your early voting site and put it in the tabulator yourself, or vote there in person: hand in your absentee ballot or have it “spoiled” and get a new one. Early voting runs through Sunday, Nov. 1.

Where do I drop it off on Election Day

On Tuesday, Nov. 3, bring your completed ballot to your polling place and put it in the tabulator, or vote there in person: hand in your absentee ballot or have it “spoiled” and get a new one. Your clerk’s drop box also takes it until 8 p.m.

More FAQ on Absentee Voting
When does my ballot have to arrive?

By 8 p.m. on Election Day, Tuesday, Nov. 3, at your clerk’s office.

Can I vote in person instead?

Yes, at your early voting site or your polling place on Nov. 3, if you haven’t already returned it. Hand in your voted absentee ballot there, or have it “spoiled” and get a new ballot.

Who can return my ballot for me?

You; a member of your immediate family or someone in your household you asked; a mail carrier; or an election official. It is a felony for anyone else to possess or deliver your ballot.

I made a mistake or signed in the wrong place.

Ask your clerk for a new ballot. Sign the envelope the way you sign your driver’s license, state ID or voter registration.

How do I know my ballot arrived?

Track it at Michigan.gov/Vote.

Can I change my vote after returning it?

Only with a written, signed request that reaches your clerk by 5 p.m. on Friday, Oct. 23.
