Have you returned your absentee ballot?
It must reach your clerk by 8 p.m. on Election Day, Tue, Nov. 3.
Where to mail
Mail by Oct. 20
It must arrive by 8 p.m., Nov. 3
  • Sign and date the envelope as on your driver’s license or state ID.
  • Use the prepaid return envelope.
  • After Oct. 20, use a drop box or your clerk’s office.
Where is my drop box
Open until 8 p.m., Nov. 3
  • Your clerk’s secure drop box.
Where is my clerk’s office
  • Hand in your signed envelope in person.
  • Open office hours and the weekend before Election Day.
Where is my early voting location
Sat, Oct. 24 to Sun, Nov. 1
  • Put your completed ballot in the tabulator yourself.
  • Or vote in person: hand in your absentee ballot, or have it spoiled and get a new one.
Where do I drop it off on Election Day
Tue, Nov. 3
Polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  • Put your completed ballot in the tabulator at your polling place.
  • Or vote in person: hand in your absentee ballot, or have it spoiled and get a new one.
  • Or use your clerk’s drop box until 8 p.m.
More FAQ on Absentee Voting
When does my ballot have to arrive?
  • By 8 p.m., Tue, Nov. 3, at your clerk’s office.
Can I vote in person instead?
  • Yes, at early voting or your polling place on Nov. 3, if you haven’t returned it.
  • Hand in your absentee ballot, or have it spoiled and get a new one.
Who can return my ballot for me?
  • You.
  • Immediate family or someone in your household, if you ask.
  • A mail carrier.
  • An election official.
  • Anyone else holding or delivering it commits a felony.
I made a mistake or signed in the wrong place.
  • Ask your clerk for a new ballot.
  • Sign as on your driver’s license, state ID or voter registration.
How do I know my ballot arrived?
  • Call your clerk.
  • Check for updates: Michigan.gov/Vote
Can I change my vote after returning it?
  • Only by a written, signed request.
  • It must reach your clerk by 5 p.m., Fri, Oct. 23.
  • Check for updates: Michigan Secretary of State
